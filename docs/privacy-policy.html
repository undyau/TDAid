TD Aid

Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026 · Developer contact: undyau@gmail.com

TD Aid is a tournament-day tool for disc golf Tournament Directors. Everything it stores lives on your own device — there is no TD Aid server, and nothing you enter is sold or shared for advertising.

What TD Aid does

TD Aid helps a Tournament Director run an event day-of: pulling the live roster, tee times and scores from the PDGA and the ADG Tour, running an announcing queue, sending on-device tee-time alerts, and keeping notes on players (pronunciation, sponsor, walk-on song, check-in status).

Information TD Aid handles

PDGA sign-in Sent to PDGA only

If you connect your PDGA account, the username and password you type are sent directly to PDGA's own servers at pdga.com to sign you in — the same request your browser would make. TD Aid never stores your password. It saves the session token PDGA returns, plus your username, in local app storage on your device so you're not asked to sign in again every time you open the app. This stays on your device until you sign out or clear the app's storage.

Tournament & player data Read-only, public

Names, PDGA numbers, ratings, schedules and live scores are fetched from PDGA and the ADG Tour's public tournament pages for the event you select, then cached on your device so Field Mode stays fast on a weak signal.

Notes you add Stays on device

Pronunciation notes, sponsor, walk-on song and check-in status are either typed by you in the app or imported from a CSV file you choose. These are stored only in the app's local database and are never transmitted anywhere.

Tee-time alerts On-device only

Tee times are used to schedule Android alarms and notifications on your own phone. No alert or schedule data leaves your device.

What TD Aid doesn't do

  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No TD Aid account system, and no TD Aid servers or database — the app talks only to PDGA and the ADG Tour, and only for the reasons above
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with third parties

Where your data lives, and how to remove it

Everything TD Aid stores — cached tournament data, your notes, and your saved PDGA sign-in — lives in the app's local database and preferences on your device. You can disconnect PDGA or ADG at any time from the app's Data Sources screen. Uninstalling TD Aid, or clearing its storage from Android's App Info screen, deletes all of it immediately.
